# Natura Wellness foundations

## The brand

Quiet sage: a light, clean look where the product and the words do the talking. Surfaces stay calm and
text stays easy to read.

Headings and body copy are set in Plus Jakarta Sans, a friendly geometric sans that keeps the page warm
without getting loud.

## How themes work

The semantic tokens (`bg`, `fg`, `surface`, `decor`, and the rest) are the contract. Components only ever
use the utilities those tokens generate, never a raw color. Putting `data-theme` on a wrapper switches
the tokens for everything inside it, so a whole subtree can change theme without touching a component.

## Where tokens live

Everything is declared in `src/styles/globals.css`: the `--brand-*` primitives, the semantic tokens and
the `[data-theme]` blocks. The toolbar's theme picker applies a theme to the story you are looking at.
